Things to Do

At Brasada Ranch

Pools & Poolside Bar

The Athletic Center has the resort pools, the Sundance Poolside Bar & Grill, and bike and equipment rentals.

Spa Brasada

Treat yourself to a massage or facial before the big day.

Golf

Play a round at Brasada Canyons, or warm up at the driving range.

Tennis & Pickleball

Courts are right next to the Athletic Center.

Around Central Oregon

Smith Rock State Park

Dramatic canyon hiking along the Crooked River. The Misery Ridge trail rewards you with big views.

About 40 minutes from Brasada Ranch

Downtown Bend & the Old Mill District

Shops, restaurants, and riverside walking paths along the Deschutes River.

About 35 minutes from Brasada Ranch

Bend Ale Trail

Bend is known for its breweries. Pick up a passport and sample your way around town.

About 35 minutes from Brasada Ranch

Tumalo Falls

A short walk to a 97 foot waterfall in the Deschutes National Forest.

About an hour from Brasada Ranch
