FAQ

What's the dress code?

Formal attire. The ceremony is outdoors in the high desert. Grab your bolo tie and boots if you have them. Evenings can get chilly, so make sure to bring a jacket, fur, or extra layer in case.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation will indicate whether your plus-one is included. If you have questions, feel free to reach out before RSVPing.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, but we're keeping the ceremony and rehearsal adults only. If you'd like, you can use Snappy Sitters, a babysitting service we recommend.

Where should I stay?

We recommend staying on-site at Brasada Ranch. To receive these rates, guests must book using your customized booking link or by calling 1-877-771-4239 and referencing the "Podmore/Wolf Wedding."

What about transportation?

Rental cars are available at the airport, though not necessary if you're staying at Brasada Ranch. Uber is also available and it's about a 15-minute ride from the airport. If you'd like to get around the resort itself, golf carts can be rented on-site.
