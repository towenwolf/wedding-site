FAQ

What's the dress code?

Formal attire. The ceremony is outdoors in the high desert, so we recommend layers for the evening and comfortable footwear for the terrain.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation will indicate whether your plus-one is included. If you have questions, feel free to reach out before RSVPing.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, but we're keeping the ceremony and rehearsal adults only. There are babysitting services available locally, and we're happy to share recommendations if you'd like.

Where should I stay?

We recommend staying on-site at Brasada Resort. To receive these rates, guests must book a minimum of two nights using your customized booking link or by calling the resort and referencing the "Podmore/Wolf Wedding."

Should I rent a car?

Rental cars are available at the airport, though not necessary if you're staying at Brasada Resort.

Can I take an Uber?

Yes, Uber is available and it's about a 15-minute ride from the airport.
