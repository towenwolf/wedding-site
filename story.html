Our Story

We met as friends at a music festival at the Gorge Amphitheater in the summer of 2018, through our college buddy, Mac. Travis was traveling in from Dallas, and Diamond was living in Portland. We stayed in touch as friends for a year and a half, furthering our bond by many deep conversations, having a shared sense of humor, a love for music, concerts, and the outdoors.

Our first real date came in December of 2019, when Travis flew out to visit Diamond in Portland for the first time. We grabbed banh mi at the Golden Dragon in Southeast Portland, and that same weekend we went skiing together, where Travis met Diamond's family. From there, we started a long distance relationship, then COVID hit. Travis finished grad school Spring of 2020 and Diamond had lost her job at Adidas. Even though the circumstances weren't ideal for the remainder of that year, it really pushed us closer together. Travis found a job and moved to Portland in the summer of 2021, where we then made the decision to move in together where we could finally start building a life together in the same place.

Since then, we've made Oregon our home. We love our day dates at the rose garden, where we literally stop and smell the roses. We love to cook together and have even started venturing into taking cooking classes in the city. When we aren't cooking, we are either exploring a new restaurant, getting tacos at our favorite local taco truck El Mas Chignon, or picking up the best chicken shawarma and hummus from UR Mediterranean. We love exploring the PNW and going on adventures together. Some of our favorite things to do here are going on weekend trips to the coast, the mountain, and central Oregon. Long drives are filled with deep conversation, lots of laughs, and impromptu karaoke and serenades by Diamond. We've been fortunate enough to travel together as well, across the country and international. We've learned we make a great team.

The proposal happened on May 01, 2026, at the Oregon coast. Travis took Diamond to the picturesque Elk Flats, where he got down on one knee and asked for her hand in marriage. Of course, Diamond said yes. We then rounded out the weekend with lots of delicious wine and a seafood boil with our local friends and family.

Now we can't wait to gather everyone we love in Powell Butte, Oregon, to celebrate this next chapter and make even more memories with all of you. Thank you for being part of our story.
