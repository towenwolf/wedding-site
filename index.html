Our Story

[ photo here ]

We met the way good things happen in Central Oregon — slowly, honestly, out in the open air. What started as a shared laugh turned into long drives down empty roads, campfires under Cascade skies, and years of adventures neither of us could have predicted.

On a quiet evening last autumn, with the stars wide open overhead, the question was finally asked. She said yes before he finished the sentence.

We can't wait to celebrate with everyone we love, right here in the place we call home.
